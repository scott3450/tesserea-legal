Questions that come up

How does it work?

Each time you do a habit, its stone comes to the bench. Lift it and set it in its place on the work; it only settles when it is right over its bed. When the last stone is set, the joints are drawn and the work hangs in your gallery.

I missed a day.

Nothing is lost. The work waits for you, half made, and there is no streak to break. If you did the habit but did not open Tesserea, yesterday's stones stay on the bench, faded, until noon the next day.

When does a new day start?

At 4 a.m. A stone set at half past midnight still counts for the day before.

I set the wrong stone.

Press and hold a stone you set today and it goes back to the bench. Stones from earlier days stay where they are.

Why can I only add one habit?

New habits open one at a time, with each of your first works. If you would rather start with all of them, turn on Settings ▸ Skip the path.

Do I need an account?

No. Tesserea works fully without one, and everything stays on your iPhone. If you sign in with Apple or Google (Settings ▸ Account), your habits and works are also kept in your account, so they survive deleting the app and appear on your other iPhones.

I am moving to a new iPhone.

If you are signed in, sign in with the same account on the new iPhone and your works come back. Without an account, your habits and works are part of your iPhone backup: restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start. Without an account, deleting the app deletes your works.

How do I delete my account?

Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account. Your account and everything kept in it are deleted. You choose whether the works on this iPhone stay or go too.

What does Tesserea Plus add?

Up to six habits, and every work in the collection, with the works still to come. Without it you keep the first path, three habits and the first three works. The stones you have set and the works you have finished are yours: they are never locked, even if the subscription ends. Stones are never sold; they only come from habits you keep.

How do I cancel or restore Tesserea Plus?

Cancel in your iPhone's Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the current period ends. To bring a subscription back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone, use Restore Purchases in Tesserea, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The widget is empty.

The widget shows a picture that Tesserea saves when you leave the app. Open Tesserea once and the widget follows.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Tesserea keeps your habits and the stones you set. Without an account, all of it stays on your iPhone. An account is optional; if you sign in, the data listed below is also kept in your account so it can reach your other iPhones.

What Tesserea stores

  • Your habits: name, what they follow, how many stones a day, how many days a week.
  • Each stone you set: the day, the habit and the work it went into.
  • Your works: which ones you finished, and the names you gave them.
  • Your settings: evening note and its time, sound, the path.
  • Whether you have an active subscription.

Where it is stored

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. A picture of the work you are making is also saved to a shared app group container so the widget can draw it. That container is on your iPhone too, and only Tesserea and its widget can read it. Like any app's data, it is included in your iPhone backup if you back up your device.

What leaves your iPhone

Without an account, nothing.

With an account (Sign in with Apple or Google), Tesserea sends:

  • Your email address and an account identifier, as Apple or Google give them. With Apple you can hide your email; Tesserea then receives a private relay address.
  • Your habits, your works and each stone you set, as listed above.

Your settings and your subscription status are not sent. Tesserea does not ask Apple or Google for your name, contacts or anything else.

The account and its data are kept with Supabase, the service that runs Tesserea's sign-in and database, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can read only its own data. Supabase processes it on Tesserea's behalf, only to provide this service, under its data processing terms.

When you sign in with Google, Google's own sign-in page opens in a browser sheet; Google's privacy policy applies to that page. Sign in with Apple uses your iPhone's own sheet.

There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Tesserea does not track you across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Tesserea never sees your payment details, name or email address. To know whether a subscription is active, Tesserea asks the App Store on your iPhone, which verifies it there. The purchase record itself is kept by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

The evening note is a local notification, scheduled and delivered by your iPhone; no notification server is involved.

Sharing

When you share a finished work, Tesserea draws a picture of it and hands it to the share sheet you chose. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

How long it is kept

On your iPhone, until you delete it. In your account, for as long as the account exists.

Deleting your data

Archive a habit from its screen; its stones stay in your ledger. Deleting Tesserea removes everything it stored on your iPhone, including the widget picture.

To delete your account, open Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete account. The account and everything kept in it are deleted from the server at once, and for an Apple account, Tesserea's access to your Apple ID is revoked. You choose whether the works on this iPhone stay or are deleted too. Signing out deletes nothing. If you no longer have the app, write to the address below and the account will be deleted.

Children

Tesserea needs no personal information to work. It collects only what is listed above, and only if you choose to sign in.

Changes

Subscription

  • Tesserea Plus is offered monthly and yearly. The price and length are shown in the app before you buy.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • It renews automatically for the same length and price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before that period ends.
